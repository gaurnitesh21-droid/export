Consistency through joint ventures

One manufacturing objective.
Shared quality expectations.

The value of a strong joint venture goes beyond production capacity. It creates an opportunity to coordinate product specifications, repeat-order consistency and clear technical expectations between manufacturing and export teams.

For salt, our quality focus includes composition, moisture, granulation and applicable iodine parameters. For emery stones, it includes dimensions, abrasive characteristics, curing or strength considerations where applicable, and compatibility with milling equipment.

Final inspection procedures, technical tolerances, certifications and quality documentation depend on the specific manufacturing and transaction agreement. We avoid unsupported quality guarantees.
