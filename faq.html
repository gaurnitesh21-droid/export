Frequently asked questions

Is Prime Reach Exporters a manufacturer or an exporter?

Prime Reach Exporters is an India-based export company operating through joint venture manufacturing arrangements with specialized manufacturers. Production takes place at the relevant partner facilities, with Prime Reach Exporters coordinating agreed specifications, quality oversight and international export operations.

What does your joint venture manufacturing model mean?

Our model brings together the production capabilities of manufacturing partners with export-market requirements coordinated by Prime Reach Exporters. Ownership, production responsibilities and supervision arrangements follow the applicable joint venture agreements.

Do you own the manufacturing facilities?

Our operating model focuses on joint venture manufacturing relationships. Factory ownership and operational responsibilities depend on the specific legal and commercial arrangements; we do not present every partner facility as company-owned.

How do you manage product quality and consistency?

We coordinate clearly defined specifications, applicable production requirements, agreed inspection checkpoints and quality verification with manufacturing partners. The scope of supervision and testing is confirmed for each order.

Can products be manufactured to custom specifications?

We welcome custom grade, dimension, composition and packaging requirements. The relevant manufacturing partner reviews technical feasibility, production capabilities and mutually agreed commercial terms.

What salt products do you offer?

Our salt range includes iodized, non-iodized, raw, industrial, black (Kala Namak), rock (Sendha Namak), refined and specialty grades, subject to the agreed production and product specifications.

What types of flour mill emery stones do you offer?

We focus on Danish Vertical Type, Danish Horizontal Type, Indian Vertical Type and Indian Horizontal Type emery grinding stones for compatible flour milling equipment.

Can you manufacture customized emery stone sizes?

We can discuss custom stone dimensions and technical requirements through the relevant manufacturing arrangements. Please share outer diameter, thickness, bore and mounting geometry, machine details and any drawings or photographs.

Can you provide testing or inspection documents?

Relevant product analysis, inspection reports and transaction documentation may be coordinated according to the product and agreed requirements. No certification or report should be assumed until confirmed in writing.

Can you support consistent repeat orders?

We aim to support repeat-order consistency through established product specifications, manufacturing communication and agreed quality verification. Actual tolerances and acceptance criteria are confirmed in each applicable order agreement.

Do you offer private-label or customized packaging?

Buyer-specific packaging, labeling and private-label requests can be evaluated according to the manufacturing partner's capability, product characteristics, quantity and destination-market rules.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities depend on product type, manufacturing process, specifications, packing format and commercial requirements. Please send an enquiry for a product-specific discussion.

Which international markets do you serve?

We welcome enquiries internationally, with business development focused on Africa, the Middle East, South Asia and other regions, subject to trade requirements and transaction feasibility.

Do you offer FOB, CFR or CIF quotations?

These trade terms can be discussed according to the product and shipment. The applicable Incoterms® rule, named location and responsibilities will be confirmed in the written commercial agreement.

How can we request a quotation?

Provide the product name, required grade or technical dimensions, quantity, packaging preferences, destination port or country and any relevant quality or documentation requirements. Contact us at sales@primereachexporters.in or +91 9694681337 / +91 9660014076.
