How Prime Reach Exporters handles business enquiries, product specifications and manufacturing-related commercial information.

Draft for review — this page must be aligned with the final website hosting, analytics, enquiry processing and applicable law before public launch.

Information you provide

Business enquiries may include your name, company information, contact details, destination country, product specifications, technical drawings and commercial requirements. The current static website does not store or automatically submit enquiry form data to a Prime Reach Exporters server.

How the enquiry tools work

When you choose WhatsApp, the website prepares an enquiry for your review before you send it. The email option opens your mail app addressed to sales@primereachexporters.in; copying the enquiry uses your device clipboard. These external services have their own privacy practices.

Information use and partner coordination

Details you voluntarily send may be used to evaluate product requirements, discuss joint venture manufacturing feasibility, coordinate with the relevant manufacturing partners, prepare quotations and communicate regarding prospective transactions. Appropriate sharing of technical details may be necessary for these purposes.

Other website data

This site uses externally hosted fonts and demonstration images, which may involve network requests to the relevant third parties. The deployed website may require an updated notice concerning hosting logs, analytics, form providers or cookies if those features are added.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Prime Reach Exporters at sales@primereachexporters.in or through the phone and WhatsApp details on the Contact page.
