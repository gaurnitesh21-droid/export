Information about how the website handles business enquiry details.

Draft for review — this page must be aligned with the final website hosting, analytics, enquiry processing and applicable law before public launch.

Information you provide

Business enquiry information may include your name, organization, contact details, destination country and product requirements. The current website preview does not transmit this information to a Prime Reach Exports server.

How the enquiry tools work

When you choose WhatsApp, the website opens the WhatsApp service with an enquiry message you can review and send yourself. When you select email, it opens your email app; when you copy the enquiry, it uses your device clipboard. These services may apply their own privacy terms.

Other website data

This site uses externally hosted fonts and demonstration images, which may involve network requests to the relevant third parties. The deployed website may require an updated notice concerning hosting logs, analytics, form providers or cookies if those features are added.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact Prime Reach Exports using the phone or WhatsApp details on the Contact page.
