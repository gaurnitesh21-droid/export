Technical compatibility

Precision before
procurement.

Emery grinding stones are not interchangeable solely by appearance. Geometry, composition, material compatibility and operating requirements can differ across machines.

We encourage buyers to share complete machine or replacement-stone details before confirming an order. Final suitability should be approved by the machine manufacturer or qualified technician.
